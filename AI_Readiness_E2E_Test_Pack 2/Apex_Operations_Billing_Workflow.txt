Apex Operations Billing Reconciliation Workflow - Synthetic Test Data

Current Process:
1. Delivery teams export weekly time entries from project trackers.
2. Finance downloads Excel logs and manually maps hours against invoice templates.
3. Client-specific billing terms are checked in historic proposal documents.
4. Exceptions are escalated over Slack and email.
5. Final invoice notes are reviewed by operations manager.

Pain Points:
- Manual spreadsheet mapping takes 6-8 staff hours per week.
- Billing descriptions are inconsistent across clients.
- Contract terms are checked manually and sometimes missed.
- Commercial figures cannot be generated directly by AI without human validation.

AI Opportunity:
Billing Excel Extraction Agent can extract rows, normalize labels, compare against contract terms, and produce discrepancy summaries. It should remain a decision-support assistant, not an autonomous billing engine.

Governance Requirement:
Any billing-related output below 95% confidence must be escalated to finance review. All source documents and assumptions must be visible in the evidence trail.
